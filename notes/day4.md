# Day 4 Understanding

Eigenvectors represent important directions in data.
Eigenvalues measure how much variance exists along those directions.
Covariance measures how two variables change together.
PCA reduces dimensions while preserving important information.
Machine learning often works better with reduced noise and compressed features.
Probability helps quantify uncertainty in predictions and data behavior.
Distributions describe how values are spread across datasets.
Variance measures how much data changes from the mean.
Linear algebra and probability together form the mathematical foundation of ML.
Understanding variance and covariance improves intuition about feature relationships.

## Mathematical Connections

Mean measures data center.
Variance measures spread.
Covariance measures relationships between variables.
Eigenvectors represent important stable directions.
Eigenvalues measure importance of those directions.
PCA uses covariance matrices and eigenvectors to reduce dimensions while preserving important variance.
